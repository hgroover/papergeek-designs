Pre-launch checklist

Top

1. Parachute should be gently held from the middle so all the shroud lines go downward
2. Fold the parachute once
3. Wrap the shroud lines around the folded parachute
4. Wad 2 pieces of recovery wadding and stuff them down the tube
5. Push the parachute into the tube so there is a little space remaining at the top
6. Stuff the shock cord into the tube
7. Put the nose cone into the tube

Bottom 

8. Remove the engine retainer and any spent engine
9. Insert engine with nozzle downward
10. Screw engine retainer so it is not tight but will hold the engine in place
11. Verify that all fins and other pieces are intact and the rocket is ready for flight

On the pad

12. Insert igniter all the way into the engine nozzle
13. Insert the plastic wedge that holds the igniter in place
14. Put the rocket on the launch rod through both launch lugs
15. Launch rod should be pointing straight up. If windy, it should be angled slightly INTO the wind.
16. Attach the launch controller clips to the igniter leads. Make sure they do not cross!
17. Make sure the launch rod end cap has been removed

Launch preparation

18. Make sure the launch area is clear in a 20 foot radius (minimum)
19. Unlock and arm the launch controller
20. Countdown and launch
21. Disarm the launch controller and remove the key
