Model rocket assembly

1. Lightly screw the engine retainer onto the engine mount. This goes on the end of the rocket and will remain outside the fuselage.
2. Apply a light bead of glue around the engine mount. Note that there are "glue grooves" to allow additional glue to be put so that a light coating ends up on the inside.
3. Carefully slide the engine mount into the bottom of the fuselage (the end away from the shock cord)
4. Carefully unscrew the engine retainer to make sure there is no glue holding it in place.
5. Apply a light coating of glue around the bottom of the fuselage for the fin can
6. Carefully slide the fin can in place so that its bottom edge is flush with the fuselage tube.
7. Using the launch rod, align the upper launch lug with the bottom launch lug integrated into the fin can.
8. Apply a light bead of glue to the curved part of the upper launch lug.
9. Press the upper launch lug in place, and use the launch rod to make sure it's aligned.
10. Use a rubber band to hold the upper launch lug in place while the glue dries. Check again to make sure it's aligned.

WAIT 1 - glue needs to set 30 minutes before continuing. Assemble parachute.

101. Apply self-adhesive reinforcements to all 8 holes in the parachute.
102. For each pair of parachute holes, tie the end of a string piece (pre-measured) to each hole using a square knot. Do not tighten so the parachute plastic crumples but make sure the knot is tight.
103. Gather together the 4 parachute shroud loops and tie a single overhand knot
104. Loop the shock cord through the bar in the bottom of the nose cone with about 4 inches to spare. Tie an overhand knot and tighten.
105. Tie the remaining end of the shock cord to the parachute shroud lines using a square knot. Tighten!
106. Hold the parachute at the top and gently smooth it down (like folding an umbrella).
107. Fold the parachute in half so the top is almost but not quite down to the shroud holes.
108. Gently wind the shroud lines, then the shock cord, around the outside of the parachute.
109. Stuff the parachute carefully into the fuselage and make sure the nose cone fits snugly

When WAIT 1 is complete, glue should be set enough to continue. Be careful as the glue is set but not fully dry.

11. Identify the root of each fin. Make sure the fins fit into the slots on the fin can.
12. Apply a light bead of glue along the root of each fin and push them into the slots. Make sure the tops of the fins are snug against the top of the fin can.
13. Rocket can be gently rested on the fins while glue dries. Allow glue to dry for at least 2 hours before launch.
